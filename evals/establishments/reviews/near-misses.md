# Review: near-miss items

Three independent reviewers, one per jurisdiction, each given the near-miss document, the establishment's other documents, the checklist item and its regulation text: a US retail food inspector and attorney (Food Code 2026), a Brazilian health surveillance inspector and lawyer (RDC 216/2004) and a Portuguese ASAE inspector and lawyer (Regulation 852/2004). Result: 4 correct, 0 wrong, 0 ambiguous.

| Bakery Austin | us.backflow-prevention | insufficient_evidence | correct | 5-203.14 requires an air gap or an approved device "at each point of use at the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT", and 5-205.12(A) forbids creating a cross connection. The only backflow record tests an RP assembly at "880 Cedar Elm Street, Building 2", while the permit, the pest report and the report's own mailing address put the bakery at 1207 Mesquite Bend Road, Suite B, and no other document mentions air gaps, devices or cross connections for the espresso or dish machine. ## Changes made after the review

- Bistro São Paulo: the report's line "Os resultados referem-se exclusivamente à amostra analisada.", its strongest near-miss sentence, was added to the item's `nearMiss` quotes.
- Bistro Lisboa: the item's note now rests on the special-cases exception and the Portuguese rule, not only on the terrace rules' statement that no food is prepared or stored there, which skips the "handled" limb.

## Realism and naming

- Backflow test report: tester licence, gauge accuracy date, plausible RP readings and a copy to the utility's cross-connection program match Texas practice; a real TCEQ-20700 form would also name the manufacturer, model, water system and the tester's signature. The wrong site reads as a billing or copy-paste mix-up, and no other document mentions a second site. File name and title are neutral; the row label "(location of the assembly)" is a mild, fair pointer.
- Ice report: report number, sample identification with supplier and lot, collection in a sterile bottle, the usual potability parameters and limits, and the standard sample-only disclaimer; method, analysis dates, accreditation and the chemist's CRQ number are missing, a minor gap. File name and title are neutral, and the 2024 dates appear only in the body.
- Terrace rules: Lisbon terraces commonly post pet rules, and its licence number matches the establishment register. The scope sentence is a little convenient but plausible. File name and title state the scope without hinting at a verdict; the "ANIMAIS DE COMPANHIA" heading is the lure. The main risk is a judge that reads "handled" broadly, misses the exception and answers `non_compliant`.
