Allergen control procedure - Bakery Austin

Fictional document for the Normetry demo. It does not describe a real business.

Allergens handled here: wheat, milk, eggs, soy, tree nuts (almonds, pecans) and sesame.

1. Every recipe card lists its allergens. A new or changed recipe is not baked until its card is updated.
2. Nut products are made on Mondays and Thursdays only, on the marked table, with their own tools.
3. After a nut product, the table, mixer and tools are washed, rinsed and sanitized before any other product.
4. Labels on packaged items name every allergen in the recipe.
5. When a customer asks about allergens, staff read the recipe card. Staff never guess.

Training: every new hire reads this procedure in their first week and signs the training log.

Owner: Bruna Owner. Reviewed every year or when a recipe changes.
