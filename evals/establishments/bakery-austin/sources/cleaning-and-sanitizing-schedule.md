# Bakery Austin - Cleaning and Sanitizing Schedule

Fictional document for demonstration: it describes no real business.

Version 3, effective 2026-07-01. Owner of this schedule: Dana Whitfield, general manager. The person in charge signs the daily closing checklist, and the general manager signs the weekly and monthly tasks below.

## Sanitizer

The bakery uses a quaternary ammonium (quat) sanitizer. The label concentration is 200 to 400 ppm with a contact time of 60 seconds. Each new batch of sanitizer at the three-compartment sink and in the sanitizer buckets is tested with quat test strips. The three-compartment sink reading is written in the Sanitizer and Warewashing Log every morning. A reading below 200 ppm means the solution is remade and tested again before anything is sanitized in it.

## Food-contact surfaces

Food-contact surfaces and utensils used with TCS food are washed, rinsed and sanitized after each task, at least every 4 hours while in use, and whenever a surface used for raw eggs, raw sausage or raw chicken is next used for ready-to-eat food.

| Surface or equipment | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Pastry table, prep tables, sandwich counter | After each task and at least every 4 hours | Wash, rinse, sanitize, air dry |
| Cutting boards and knives | Between raw and ready-to-eat use, and every 4 hours | Dish machine or three-compartment sink |
| Mixers, bowls, dough hooks, sheeter | After each use and between nut and nut-free batches | Wash, rinse, sanitize, air dry |
| Steam table pans, soup well inserts | At closing | Dish machine |
| Espresso machine steam wands | After each drink | Wipe with a clean cloth and purge |

## Warewashing

Three-compartment sink: wash in detergent at 110°F (43°C) or above, rinse in clean water, sanitize in quat at 200 to 400 ppm for 60 seconds, then air dry on the drainboard.

Dish machine: a high-temperature door-type machine (stationary rack, dual temperature). The wash tank runs at 150°F (66°C) or above, and the final sanitizing rinse at the manifold is 180°F to 194°F (82°C to 90°C). Once a day, an irreversible 160°F (71°C) temperature label is run through the machine on a plate to confirm the utensil surface temperature. The wash and final rinse temperatures and the label result go in the Sanitizer and Warewashing Log.

## Wiping cloths

Wet wiping cloths are kept in a sanitizer bucket between uses. The buckets are remade every 4 hours, or sooner when the water is dirty. Dry cloths are kept dry and used only for drying. All cloths are sent to the linen service every day and come back laundered.

## Non-food-contact surfaces and premises

| Area | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen and dish area floors | Sweep and mop at closing | Scrub with a deck brush | Clean under the ovens and racks |
| Walls and doors | Spot clean splashes | Wash kitchen walls | Wash the dry storage walls |
| Walk-in cooler | Wipe handles and door gaskets | Clean floor and shelves | Empty and clean the whole box |
| Hood and filters | Wipe the outside | Wash the filters | Contractor cleans the ducts every 6 months |
| Dry storage | Sweep | Clean under the shelves | Wash the shelves |
| Restrooms | Clean at 11:00 am and at closing | Descale fixtures | Wash the walls |
| Café tables and chairs | After each use and at closing | Wash the chair legs | Wash the window ledges |

## Waste and refuse

Indoor trash cans have lids and liners, are emptied at least at closing, and are washed every week. The outdoor dumpster and the recycling container are leakproof and have tight-fitting lids that are kept closed. The dumpster pad is swept every day and hosed down every week.

Trash and recycling are picked up by our contract hauler, Arroyo Hauling, on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, and the hauler adds a pickup when the dumpster is full before the next scheduled day.
