# Review: FDA Food Code 2026 inspection checklist

Independent reviewer briefed as a US attorney specialized in retail food safety law and the FDA Food Code, given only the parsed text of the FDA Food Code 2026 and the 45 checklist items. Result: 33 correct, 4 wrong, 8 ambiguous.

| id | verdict | reason |
|---|---|---|
| us.certified-person-in-charge | correct | 2-101.11(A) requires a person in charge "present at the food establishment during all hours of operation" (Pf) and 2-102.12(A) says "The person in charge shall be a certified food protection manager", so together they state the title. The minimal-risk exemptions (2-101.11(C), 2-102.12(B)) need a regulatory designation a restaurant rarely gets; major fits the Pf marker. |
| us.active-managerial-control | correct | 2-103.11(D), (E), (G), (H), (I) and (L) put each listed step (handwashing, receiving, cooking, cooling, hot and cold holding, sanitizing) under the person in charge's routine monitoring, all Pf, so major fits. |
| us.employee-health-reporting | correct | 2-103.11(P) requires employees to be "informed in a verifiable manner" of the duty to report and 2-201.11(A) and (E) require them to report symptoms and diagnoses to the person in charge. The (A) list is marked P, so critical holds; the title leaves out the exposure history in (A)(4)-(5) but states nothing wrong. |
| us.ill-employee-exclusion | correct | 2-201.12 opens "The person in charge shall exclude or restrict a food employee" and lists the symptoms and diagnoses, every paragraph marked P. |
| us.employee-illness-policy | correct | 2-501.11(A) requires "a written employee illness policy" and (B)(1)-(3) list symptoms, diagnoses, and "exclusion and/or restriction and reinstatement", all Pf. |
| us.vomit-diarrhea-cleanup | correct | 2-502.11: "A food establishment shall have written procedures for employees to follow when responding to vomiting or diarrheal events" (Pf). |
| us.handwashing | correct | 2-301.12(A) sets "at least 20 seconds" (P); 2-301.14(B) "After using the toilet room" and (F) "when changing tasks" (P) cover the rest of the title. |
| us.no-bare-hand-contact | wrong | 3-301.11(B) applies to "exposed, ready-to-eat food" and opens "Except when washing fruits and vegetables ... or as specified in ¶¶ (D) and (E)": (D) allows bare-hand contact with an ingredient added to food that will be cooked, and (E) allows it with prior approval and written procedures. "Never" contradicts both. Fix: "Exposed RTE food is not touched with bare hands, except under 3-301.11(D) or an approved (E) plan". |
| us.handwashing-sinks | wrong | 5-205.11(A)-(B) and 6-301.11 support access, handwashing-only use and soap, but 6-301.12 allows disposable towels, a continuous towel system, "A heated-air hand drying device" or an air-knife dryer, so "towels" adds a requirement the Code does not impose. Fix: "... with soap and towels or a hand dryer". |
| us.approved-source | correct | 3-201.11(A) "obtained from sources that comply with law" and (B) "Food prepared in a private home may not be used", both P. No theme fits exactly; cross-contamination (adulteration) is the closest of the eight. |
| us.receiving-temperatures | ambiguous | 3-202.11(A) (P) and (E) (Pf) state the rule, but (A) opens "Except as specified in ¶ (B)", which lets food whose temperature is "specified in law governing its distribution, such as laws governing milk and molluscan shellfish" be received at that temperature. A restaurant receiving shellstock or milk relies on it, and the title as written would flag a compliant delivery. Fix: add "unless law sets another (milk, shellstock)". |
| us.shellfish-fish-records | correct | 3-203.12(C) keeps shellfish tags "for 90 calendar days from the date that is recorded on the tag" and 3-402.12(A) keeps fish freezing records "for 90 calendar days beyond the time of service or sale" (both Pf). The fish records apply only when raw or undercooked fish is served, and a supplier statement can stand in for them (3-402.12(B)); the hint covers both. |
| us.raw-rte-separation | correct | 3-302.11(A)(1) requires "separating raw animal foods during storage, preparation, holding, and display from" raw and cooked ready-to-eat food (P). |
| us.food-contact-cleaning | ambiguous | 4-602.11(A)(2) (raw to RTE change, P) and 4-703.11 (sanitize after cleaning, P) support the first half, but the four-hour rule in 4-602.11(C) applies only "if used with time/temperature control for safety food" and (D) lists seven exceptions (refrigerated prep rooms, containers held at temperature, and others). The title applies four hours to every food-contact surface. Fix: "... and every 4 h with TCS food". |
| us.sanitizer-concentration | correct | 4-501.114 (folded into 4-501.19) requires chemical sanitizer "used in accordance with the EPA registered label use instructions" with concentration criteria marked P; 4-501.116(A) (same text) and 4-302.14(A) require a test kit (Pf). Critical follows the P concentration rule. |
| us.cooking-temperatures | correct | 3-401.11(A) requires raw animal foods to be cooked "to heat all parts of the food to a temperature and for a time" from its table (P). The title does not restate the numbers, so it misstates none, and the (D) consumer-request path is covered by us.consumer-advisory. |
| us.reheating | wrong | 3-403.11(A) sets 74°C (165°F) "for 15 seconds" only for food "that is cooked, cooled, and reheated", while (C) sets "at least 57°C (135°F)" for commercially processed, packaged ready-to-eat food. Restaurants reheat such food often, so the title imposes 74°C where the Code requires 57°C and also drops the 15 seconds. Fix: "Food cooked and cooled on site is reheated for hot holding to 74°C (165°F) for 15 s within 2 hours", with (C) in the hint or a separate item. |
| us.cooling | correct | 3-501.14(A)(1)-(2): "Within two hours from 57ºC (135ºF) to 21ºC (70ºF)" and "Within a total of six hours from 57ºC (135ºF) to 5ºC (41ºF) or less" (P). The four-hour rule in (B) for food made from ambient ingredients is outside the title's scope ("cooked"). |
| us.hot-holding | ambiguous | 3-501.16(A)(1) sets 57°C (135°F) (P) but in the same clause lets "roasts cooked to a temperature and for a time specified in ¶ 3-401.11(B)" be held "at 54°C (130°F) or above". A carving station is common in restaurants, and the title would flag a compliant roast. Fix: "...; roasts per 3-401.11(B) at 54°C (130°F) or above". |
| us.cold-holding | correct | 3-501.16(A)(2): "At 5°C (41°F) or less" (P). The exceptions, untreated eggs in equipment at 7°C (45°F) ambient ((B)) and liquids in specially designed equipment ((C)), are narrow, and the evidence hint (unit logs) fits. |
| us.date-marking | ambiguous | 3-501.17(A)-(B) and 3-501.18(A) (P, so critical holds) support the rule, but it covers "refrigerated" food held "at a temperature of 5ºC (41ºF) or less", and (E)-(G) exempt meal portions, shellstock, and opened plant-made deli salads, hard and semi-soft cheeses and cultured dairy. Those are routine in a restaurant walk-in, and the title would flag them. Fix: "Refrigerated RTE TCS food held over 24 hours is date marked and used or discarded within 7 days", with the (E)-(G) exemptions in the hint. |
| us.time-as-public-health-control | correct | 3-501.19(A)(1) requires "Written procedures ... prepared in advance" and (B)(3) and (C)(3) require marking the time, all Pf, so major fits. |
| us.consumer-advisory | correct | 3-603.11(A) requires the permit holder to inform consumers "by way of a disclosure and reminder" when animal food is served raw or undercooked (Pf). |
| us.toxic-materials | correct | 7-101.11 and 7-102.11 require labels and common names (Pf); 7-201.11(A)-(B) require "separating ... by spacing or partitioning" and storage "not above food" (P). The (B) carve-out for cleaners in warewashing areas still requires that they be "stored to prevent contamination of food", so "never above it" holds. |
| us.allergen-awareness | correct | 2-103.11(O) requires employees "properly trained in food safety, including food allergy awareness", defined as "describing foods identified as major food allergens and the symptoms" (Pf). 2-102.11(C)(9) covers the person in charge. |
| us.allergen-notification | ambiguous | 3-602.12(C) states the duty: "notify consumers by written notification of the presence of major food allergens as an ingredient in unpackaged food items". The paragraph carries no P or Pf marker, which by the checklist's own rule makes it Core (minor), not major. The allergen hazard can justify major, but the item departs from the marker. Fix: set minor, or keep major and record why. |
| us.allergen-labeling | correct | 3-602.11(A)-(B) require food "packaged in a food establishment" to list ingredients ((B)(2)) and "The name of the food source for each major food allergen" ((B)(5), Pf). Major fits the Pf. |
| us.thawing | ambiguous | 3-501.13(A)-(C) support the three methods (Pf), but the running-water method is "Completely submerged under running water ... at a water temperature of 21°C (70° F) or below" with enough velocity, which "cold" understates. (D) also allows "any procedure" for an RTE portion thawed for an individual order. The title reads as a complete list. Fix: "TCS food is thawed in the fridge, under running water at 21°C (70°F) or less, or as part of cooking", with (D) in the hint. |
| us.thermometers | correct | 4-302.12(A) requires thermometers "provided and readily accessible", 4-203.11 sets the accuracy (±1°C / ±2°F) and 4-502.11(B) requires calibration "in accordance with manufacturer's specifications", all Pf. |
| us.food-safety-training | correct | 2-103.11(O): "Employees are properly trained in food safety ... as it relates to their assigned duties" (Pf). |
| us.warewashing-temperatures | ambiguous | The cited text supports the temperatures: 4-501.19 manual wash 43°C (110°F), with 4-501.110 to 4-501.112 folded in. But the wash temperatures and the mechanical sanitizing rinse are Pf, and only 4-501.111 (manual immersion at 77°C (171°F)) is P, so critical overstates most of the item. "Minimums" also misses the 90°C (194°F) rinse maximum in 4-501.112(A). Fix: split into a critical hot-water sanitizing item (4-501.111, or 4-703.11(B) for the 71°C utensil surface) and a major wash and rinse item. |
| us.wiping-cloths | correct | 3-304.14(B): cloths for counters and equipment are "Held between uses in a chemical sanitizer solution" and "Laundered daily". No marker (Core), so minor fits. |
| us.facility-cleaning | correct | 4-602.13 ("at a frequency necessary to preclude accumulation of soil residues") and 6-501.12(A) ("as often as necessary to keep them clean"), both Core. |
| us.pest-control | correct | 6-501.111, folded into 6-501.19, keeps "The premises ... free of insects, rodents, and other pests" by inspecting shipments and premises, control methods ((C), Pf) and removing harborage. Major is reasonable given the Pf on control measures. |
| us.pesticide-application | wrong | 7-202.12(C) requires a certified applicator only for "A restricted use pesticide", applied by or under direct supervision of one (Pf). Other pesticides need use per the label and allowed in a food establishment ((A)(2), P), so "by certified applicators" for all pesticides is not in the text. 7-206.12 bait stations (P) is right. Fix: "Pesticides are used per label, restricted-use ones only by certified applicators; bait in covered, tamper-resistant stations" (trim to 100). |
| us.outer-openings | correct | 6-202.15(A): "outer openings ... shall be protected against the entry of insects and rodents". No marker, so minor fits. |
| us.approved-water-source | correct | 5-101.11: drinking water "from an approved source that is: (A) A public water system; or (B) A nonpublic water system ... operated according to law" (P), with standards in 5-102.11 (P). |
| us.water-testing | correct | 5-102.13: nonpublic water "sampled and tested at least annually" (Pf); 5-102.14: "The most recent sample report ... shall be retained on file" (Core). The title is conditional on a nonpublic supply. |
| us.backflow-prevention | correct | 5-203.14 requires "an air gap" or "an approved backflow prevention device" (P) and 5-205.12(A) forbids creating "a cross connection" (P). |
| us.water-device-service | correct | 5-205.13: a water treatment device or backflow preventer is "scheduled for inspection and service" and "records demonstrating inspection and service shall be maintained" (Pf). |
| us.facility-repair | correct | 6-501.11 "Physical facilities shall be maintained in good repair" and 4-501.11 equipment "in a state of repair", both Core. |
| us.sewage-disposal | correct | 5-403.11: sewage disposed through "A public sewage treatment plant" or "An individual sewage disposal system ... operated according to law" (P). |
| us.refuse-receptacles | ambiguous | 5-501.13(A) supports durable, insect- and rodent-resistant and leakproof receptacles, and 5-501.116 (folded into 5-501.19) supports cleaning. But 5-501.113 requires an inside receptacle to be covered only if it holds food residue and is "not in continuous use", or "After they are filled", so the unqualified "covered" would flag a kitchen bin in use during service. Fix: "... kept clean and covered when required". |
| us.refuse-removal | correct | 5-502.11: refuse removed "at a frequency that will minimize the development of objectionable odors and other conditions that attract or harbor insects and rodents" (Core). |
| us.valid-permit | correct | 8-301.11: no one may operate "without a valid permit" (Pf); 8-304.11(A): "Post the permit in a location ... that is conspicuous to consumers". |

## Outcome

45 items reviewed; 47 after two splits, none dropped.

- `us.no-bare-hand-contact` (wrong): title now limited to exposed RTE food, with the 3-301.11(D) and approved (E) exceptions; hint names the (E) approval and written procedures.
- `us.handwashing-sinks` (wrong): "towels" became "towels or a dryer" (6-301.12).
- `us.reheating` (wrong): title limited to food cooked and cooled on site, 74°C (165°F) for 15 s within 2 hours; the 57°C (135°F) rule for commercially processed RTE food is in the hint.
- `us.pesticide-application` (wrong): split. It now covers label use, with restricted-use pesticides only by or under a certified applicator (7-202.12); new `us.rodent-bait-stations` (critical, 7-206.12) covers bait stations.
- `us.receiving-temperatures` (ambiguous): title adds "unless law sets another (milk, shellstock)"; received-frozen moved to the hint.
- `us.food-contact-cleaning` (ambiguous): the 4-hour rule now applies only to TCS food; the 4-602.11(D) exceptions are in the hint.
- `us.hot-holding` (ambiguous): title adds 54°C (130°F) for roasts cooked per 3-401.11(B).
- `us.date-marking` (ambiguous): title limited to refrigerated RTE TCS food, "used or discarded"; the 3-501.17(E)-(G) exemptions are in the hint.
- `us.allergen-notification` (ambiguous): severity set to minor (3-602.12(C) carries no P or Pf marker).
- `us.thawing` (ambiguous): running water now at 21°C (70°F) or less; the 3-501.13(D) individual-order exception is in the hint.
- `us.warewashing-temperatures` (ambiguous): split. New `us.hot-water-sanitizing` (critical: 77°C (171°F) manual immersion per 4-501.111, 71°C (160°F) utensil surface per 4-703.11(B)); `us.warewashing-temperatures` is now major and covers wash solutions and the machine rinse, with the 90°C (194°F) maximum in the hint.
- `us.refuse-receptacles` (ambiguous): "covered" became "covered when required" (5-501.113).
