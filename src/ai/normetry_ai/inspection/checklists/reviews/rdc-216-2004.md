# Review: RDC 216/2004 inspection checklist

Independent reviewer briefed as a Brazilian lawyer specialized in sanitary law, given only the parsed text of RDC 216/2004 and the 43 checklist items. Result: 29 correct, 8 wrong, 6 ambiguous.

| id | verdict | reason |
|---|---|---|
| br.handler-health-control | correct | 4.6.1: "O controle da saúde dos manipuladores deve ser registrado e realizado de acordo com a legislação específica." The title keeps both duties and the reference to specific law (NR-7, 3.16); major fits a records duty. |
| br.ill-handler-exclusion | wrong | 4.6.2 removes only handlers whose lesions or symptoms "possam comprometer a qualidade higiênico-sanitária dos alimentos", and only "enquanto persistirem essas condições". The title drops both limits and reaches any lesion or symptom. Fix: "Handlers with lesions or illness symptoms that could compromise food safety are kept away from food preparation while these last." |
| br.handwashing | wrong | 4.6.4 also requires washing "após qualquer interrupção do serviço, após tocar materiais contaminados" and "sempre que se fizer necessário". The title's list reads as complete and leaves these occasions out. Fix: add "after any break in work, after touching contaminated materials and whenever needed". |
| br.handwashing-sinks | wrong | 4.1.14 requires "sabonete líquido inodoro anti-séptico ou sabonete líquido inodoro e produto anti-séptico", not just soap (bar soap would pass the title); it accepts "outro sistema higiênico e seguro de secagem" besides non-recycled paper towels, and wants sinks "em número suficiente" at strategic points. Fix: "Prep areas have enough dedicated handwashing sinks with odourless liquid antiseptic soap (or liquid soap plus an antiseptic), non-recycled paper towels or another hygienic drying system, and a hands-free bin." |
| br.handler-attire | wrong | The title leaves out express rules of the cited items: "não sendo permitido o uso de barba", removal of "maquiagem", and uniforms "usados exclusivamente nas dependências internas". "No jewelry" is also narrower than "todos os objetos de adorno pessoal". Fix: "Handlers wear clean uniforms changed at least daily and worn only on the premises, hair tied and covered, no beard, short unpolished nails, and no adornments or makeup." |
| br.handler-hygiene-pop | correct | 4.11.4 d) requires the POP and 4.11.8 lists its content: handwashing steps, frequency and agents, measures for lesions and symptoms, the exams and their frequency, and the training programme. The title names those four topics. |
| br.cooking-70c | wrong | 4.8.8 allows "temperaturas inferiores ... desde que as combinações de tempo e temperatura sejam suficientes". Stated as a flat minimum, the title would fail a lawful lower-temperature process. Fix: "Heat treatment brings all parts of the food to at least 70°C, or an equally safe lower time-temperature combination, checked by time and temperature." |
| br.thawing | wrong | 4.8.13 allows the microwave only "quando o alimento for submetido imediatamente à cocção", and 4.8.14 requires thawed food not used at once to stay refrigerated. The title drops both. Fix: "Frozen food is thawed under refrigeration below 5°C, or by microwave only when cooked right away; thawed food not used at once stays refrigerated and is never refrozen." |
| br.hot-holding | correct | 4.8.15: "temperatura superior a 60ºC ... por, no máximo, 6 (seis) horas". Same temperature and time limit; critical fits. |
| br.cooling | correct | 4.8.16: "reduzida de 60ºC ... a 10ºC ... em até duas horas". The figures match, and the title is no less conditional than the sentence it renders. |
| br.cold-holding | correct | 4.8.16: "inferiores a 5ºC ... ou congelado à temperatura igual ou inferior a -18ºC". Strictly below 5°C and -18°C or colder are both kept. |
| br.storage-temperature-records | correct | 4.8.18: "A temperatura de armazenamento deve ser regularmente monitorada e registrada." Major fits a records duty. |
| br.chilled-use-by | ambiguous | Accurate for 4°C or less, but 4.8.17 also requires a shorter limit "quando forem utilizadas temperaturas superiores a 4ºC e inferiores a 5ºC", a band 4.8.16 allows, and the title is silent on it. Serving chilled food past its limit is a direct hazard, so critical is arguable. Fix: add "and sooner when kept above 4°C". |
| br.date-labelling | correct | 4.8.6 (designação, data de fracionamento, prazo de validade após a abertura) and 4.8.18 and 4.9.1 (designação, data de preparo, prazo de validade) each require a name, a date and a use-by date. |
| br.receiving-inspection | correct | 4.7.3 requires inspection and approval on receipt and a temperature check for goods "que necessitem de condições especiais de conservação". The same sentence also requires the check "de armazenamento", which is outside this receiving item; no other item covers it for raw materials. |
| br.frying-oil | wrong | 180°C is right, but 4.8.11 requires replacement "imediatamente sempre que houver alteração evidente das características físico-químicas ou sensoriais, tais como aroma e sabor". "Visibly" leaves out smell and taste, and "immediately" is dropped. Fix: "Frying oil is heated to no more than 180°C and replaced at once when its smell, taste or look clearly changes (heavy foam or smoke)." |
| br.raw-ready-separation | correct | 4.8.3: "Deve-se evitar o contato direto ou indireto entre alimentos crus, semipreparados e prontos para o consumo." |
| br.raw-handler-hand-antisepsis | correct | 4.8.4: "lavagem e a anti-sepsia das mãos antes de manusear alimentos preparados". "Sanitize" renders anti-sepsia (2.2). |
| br.produce-sanitizing | ambiguous | 4.8.19 opens with "Quando aplicável"; the title makes sanitizing unconditional for every food eaten raw. The rest (products "regularizados", no residue) is right. Fix: "Where applicable, food eaten raw is sanitized ...". |
| br.serving-hand-protection | wrong | 4.10.2 joins the measures with "e": "anti-sepsia das mãos e pelo uso de utensílios ou luvas descartáveis". The title's "or" lets sanitized bare hands touch prepared food. Fix: "Servers sanitize their hands and handle prepared food with utensils or disposable gloves." |
| br.premises-equipment-cleaning | ambiguous | 4.2.4 requires the prep area to be "higienizada" (cleaning and disinfection, 2.7) "quantas vezes forem necessárias e imediatamente após o término do trabalho". The checklist renders higienização as "cleaning" throughout, but read literally the title drops disinfection and "as often as needed". Fix: "kept clean and sanitized, and prep areas sanitized as often as needed and right after work". |
| br.cleaning-records | correct | 4.2.3: cleaning and, where needed, disinfection "quando não forem realizadas rotineiramente, devem ser registradas". |
| br.sanitizer-use-storage | correct | 4.2.5: "regularizados pelo Ministério da Saúde", dilution, contact time and mode of use per the manufacturer, kept "em local reservado". "Registered" is a fair rendering of regularizados. |
| br.cleaning-pop | correct | 4.11.4 a) requires the POP and 4.11.5 lists surface, method, active agent and concentration, contact time and temperature. |
| br.pest-free | correct | 4.3.1: premises and equipment "livres de vetores e pragas urbanas", with "ações eficazes e contínuas". Critical fits. |
| br.pest-control-company | correct | 4.3.2 requires chemical control "executado por empresa especializada" and 4.11.6 a "comprovante de execução de serviço". |
| br.pest-control-pop | correct | 4.11.4 b) and 4.11.6: the POP "devem contemplar as medidas preventivas e corretivas". |
| br.insect-screens | correct | 4.1.4: "telas milimetradas" on external openings of storage and prep areas, "removíveis", and doors of those areas "dotadas de fechamento automático". Minor fits premises. |
| br.potable-water | correct | 4.4.1: only potable water; an alternative supply "atestada semestralmente mediante laudos laboratoriais". |
| br.ice-steam-potable | correct | 4.4.2 and 4.4.3 require ice for food, and steam in direct contact with food, to come from potable water. The title leaves out steam on food-contact surfaces and ice storage hygiene, but its subject is the water source. |
| br.reservoir-cleaning | ambiguous | 4.4.4 requires the tank to be "higienizado" (cleaning and disinfection, 2.7) "em um intervalo máximo de seis meses", with records. It follows the checklist's "cleaning" convention, but for an item about the six-month duty the title should not read as washing only. Fix: "cleaned and disinfected at least every six months". |
| br.reservoir-pop | correct | 4.11.4 c) requires the POP and 4.11.7 keeps it "mesmo quando realizada por empresa terceirizada", with "o certificado de execução do serviço". |
| br.premises-surfaces | ambiguous | 4.1.3 requires "revestimento liso, impermeável e lavável"; the title drops "impermeável", a separate property (unsealed concrete can be washed but is not impermeable). The shortened defect list is fine, since the section ends "dentre outros". Fix: "smooth, impermeable and washable". |
| br.equipment-maintenance-calibration | correct | 4.1.16: "manutenção programada e periódica ... e calibração dos instrumentos ... mantendo registro". Major fits a records duty. |
| br.waste-containers | correct | 4.5.1 (identified, sufficient in number and capacity) and 4.5.2 (lids "acionadas sem contato manual" in prep and storage areas). |
| br.waste-storage | correct | 4.5.3: "freqüentemente coletados e estocados em local fechado e isolado da área de preparação e armazenamento". |
| br.rejected-goods | ambiguous | 4.7.4 orders the steps: rejected or expired lots "devem ser imediatamente devolvidos ao fornecedor e, na impossibilidade," identified and stored apart. The title makes return and segregation equal options and drops "imediatamente"; the theme `waste` is also a stretch for a receiving duty. Fix: "Rejected or expired goods are returned to the supplier at once or, when that is not possible, labelled and stored apart, and given a final destination." |
| br.good-practices-manual | correct | 4.11.1: Manual and POPs "acessíveis aos funcionários envolvidos"; 2.11 lists the minimum topics. Availability to the health authority on request is left out, but an inspection tests that by itself. |
| br.pop-approval | correct | 4.11.2: sequential instructions, frequency, "nome, o cargo e ou a função dos responsáveis", "aprovados, datados e assinados pelo responsável do estabelecimento". |
| br.records-retention | correct | 2.14 defines a record as dated and naming who filled it in, and 4.11.3 sets "período mínimo de 30 (trinta) dias". The count runs from the food's preparation date, which for daily logs is the record date. |
| br.handler-training | correct | 4.6.7: "supervisionados e capacitados periodicamente em higiene pessoal, em manipulação higiênica dos alimentos e em doenças transmitidas por alimentos", "comprovada mediante documentação"; 4.11.8 adds nominal attendance records. |
| br.responsible-person | correct | 4.12.1 and 4.12.2: the person in charge is "comprovadamente submetido a curso" on contaminants, foodborne disease, hygienic handling and good practices. |
| br.supplier-criteria | correct | 4.7.1: "devem especificar os critérios para avaliação e seleção dos fornecedores de matérias-primas, ingredientes e embalagens". "Written" is the natural reading of "especificar" in a regulation that expects operations described in the Manual (2.11, 4.11.1). |

## Outcome

The checklist now has 44 items (one split). Every title stays within 100 characters, so some fixes are shorter than the reviewer's wording but keep each condition.

- `br.ill-handler-exclusion` (wrong): limited to lesions or symptoms that may affect food safety, and only while they last.
- `br.handwashing` (wrong): adds after breaks, after touching contaminated materials ("soiling") and as needed.
- `br.handwashing-sinks` (wrong): enough sinks, used only for hands, with antiseptic liquid soap and safe drying (not only paper towels).
- `br.handler-attire` (wrong): split. `br.handler-attire` keeps 4.6.3 (clean uniforms, changed at least daily, worn only on the premises); new `br.handler-grooming` covers 4.6.6 (hair tied and covered, no beard, short unpolished nails, no adornments or makeup).
- `br.cooking-70c` (wrong): allows a lower but safe time-temperature combination.
- `br.thawing` (wrong): microwave only when cooked at once; thawed food stays chilled until used and is never refrozen.
- `br.frying-oil` (wrong): replaced at once when smell, taste or look clearly changes.
- `br.serving-hand-protection` (wrong): hand antisepsis and utensils or gloves, joined by "and".
- `br.chilled-use-by` (ambiguous): adds "and sooner when kept above 4°C"; severity stays major (date marking is a priority foundation item in the FDA mapping of research.md).
- `br.produce-sanitizing` (ambiguous): adds "Where applicable".
- `br.premises-equipment-cleaning` (ambiguous): "sanitized", prep areas as often as needed and right after work.
- `br.reservoir-cleaning` (ambiguous): "cleaned and disinfected" at least every six months.
- `br.premises-surfaces` (ambiguous): adds "impermeable".
- `br.rejected-goods` (ambiguous): return at once first, otherwise labelled, kept apart and given a destination; theme moved from `waste` to `cross_contamination_allergens`.
- Missing check noted under `br.receiving-inspection` (temperature in storage, 4.7.3): added to that item's title rather than a new item, since the same sentence states both checks.
