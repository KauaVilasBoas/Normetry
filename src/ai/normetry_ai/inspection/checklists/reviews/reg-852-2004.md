# Review: Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 inspection checklist

Independent reviewer briefed as a lawyer specialized in EU food hygiene law, given only the parsed text of Regulation 852/2004 (EN and PT) and the 40 checklist items. Result: 21 correct, 7 wrong, 12 ambiguous.

| id | verdict | reason |
|---|---|---|
| eu.haccp-procedures | correct | Article 5(1) almost verbatim: "put in place, implement and maintain a permanent procedure or procedures based on the HACCP principles". Article 5(3) makes it apply to every operator after primary production, which includes a restaurant. |
| eu.haccp-critical-limits | correct | Article 5(2)(b) and (c): critical control points "at the step or steps at which control is essential" and "critical limits at critical control points". Hazard identification (point (a)) is folded in through "for the hazards found", which the evidence hint covers with the hazard analysis table. |
| eu.haccp-monitoring | correct | Article 5(2)(d): "establishing and implementing effective monitoring procedures at critical control points". |
| eu.haccp-corrective-actions | correct | Article 5(2)(e): "corrective actions when monitoring indicates that a critical control point is not under control". |
| eu.haccp-verification | correct | Article 5(2)(f): procedures "which shall be carried out regularly, to verify that the measures outlined in subparagraphs (a) to (e) are working effectively". |
| eu.haccp-records | ambiguous | Article 5(2)(g) requires documents and records "commensurate with the nature and size of the food business", and Article 5(4)(a) repeats the proportionality for the evidence given to the authority. The title drops that qualifier, which is what limits how much paperwork a small restaurant needs. |
| eu.haccp-documents-up-to-date | correct | Article 5(4)(b) ("up-to-date at all times") and the last sentence of Article 5(2) ("When any modification is made in the product, process, or any step, food business operators shall review the procedure"). "Product or process" fairly covers "or any step". |
| eu.establishment-registration | ambiguous | Article 6(2) obliges the operator to "notify the appropriate competent authority ... with a view to the registration" and to keep its information up to date; registration itself is the authority's act. "The establishment is registered" turns a duty to notify into a result the operator does not control, so a pending registration would read as a breach. |
| eu.food-hygiene-training | correct | Annex II, Chapter XII, point 1: food handlers "supervised and instructed and/or trained in food hygiene matters commensurate with their work activity"; the title keeps "instructed or trained". |
| eu.haccp-training | ambiguous | Chapter XII, point 2 covers those responsible for the Article 5(1) procedure "or for the operation of relevant guides" and requires "adequate" training. The title drops the guides group, which matters for small restaurants that apply a national guide to good hygiene practice instead of a bespoke HACCP plan. |
| eu.food-safety-culture | correct | Annex II, Chapter XIa, point 1: "establish, maintain and provide evidence of an appropriate food safety culture"; "appropriate" carries the point 3 proportionality. Chapter XIa applies to all stages (Annex II introduction). |
| eu.personal-cleanliness | ambiguous | Chapter VIII, point 1 binds "every person working in a food-handling area". The title drops "working", so it reaches visitors or delivery staff the text does not cover. |
| eu.protective-clothing | ambiguous | The same sentence of Chapter VIII, point 1 applies to every person working in a food-handling area, while the title narrows it to "food handlers" (leaving out cleaners or kitchen porters). Its severity (minor) also differs from eu.personal-cleanliness (major), split from the same sentence. |
| eu.ill-handlers-excluded | wrong | Chapter VIII, point 2 bars a person "suffering from, or being a carrier of" such a disease from handling food "or enter any food-handling area in any capacity if there is any likelihood of direct or indirect contamination". The title drops carriers, the ban on entering food-handling areas and the likelihood-of-contamination condition. |
| eu.illness-reporting | correct | Chapter VIII, point 2, second sentence: a person so affected and "likely to come into contact with food is to report immediately the illness or symptoms" to the operator. Critical is defensible: the report is what triggers the exclusion. |
| eu.hand-washing-facilities | correct | Annex II, Chapter I, point 4: washbasins "provided with hot and cold running water, materials for cleaning hands and for hygienic drying". |
| eu.cold-chain | wrong | Chapter IX, point 5 states the cold chain rule and then permits "limited periods outside temperature control ... to accommodate the practicalities of handling during preparation, transport, storage, display and service of food, provided that it does not result in a risk to health". A restaurant relies on that exception at every service, and the title drops it. |
| eu.rapid-cooling | correct | Chapter IX, point 6: foods to be "held or served at chilled temperatures" are "cooled as quickly as possible following the heat-processing stage, or final preparation stage if no heat process is applied". |
| eu.safe-thawing | correct | Chapter IX, point 7: thawing "so as to minimise the risk of growth of pathogenic microorganisms", and run-off liquid drained "where [it] may present a risk to health", which "risky" keeps. |
| eu.cold-storage-monitoring | wrong | Chapter I, point 2(d) requires storage designed to allow temperatures "to be monitored and, where necessary, recorded" (PT: "se necessário, registadas"), and Article 2(3) gives "where necessary" a legal meaning. The title makes recording unconditional. |
| eu.allergen-equipment-cleaning | ambiguous | Chapter IX, point 9 is limited to the allergens "referred to in Annex II to Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011" and requires cleaning before use with food "not containing that substance or product". "Allergenic foods" and "before other use" are broader than the text. |
| eu.food-protected-from-contamination | correct | Chapter IX, point 3: "At all stages of production, processing and distribution, food is to be protected against any contamination likely to render the food unfit". Dropping the "likely to render ..." clause does not change the duty in practice, since contamination means "the presence or introduction of a hazard" (Article 2(1)(f)). |
| eu.raw-materials-acceptance | wrong | Chapter IX, point 1 bars acceptance only where contamination is such that "even after the food business operator had hygienically applied normal sorting and/or preparatory or processing procedures, the final product would be unfit for human consumption". The title drops that threshold, so soiled vegetables that washing makes safe would count as a breach. |
| eu.raw-processed-separation | ambiguous | Chapter IX, point 5, last sentence, applies to "food businesses manufacturing, handling and wrapping processed foodstuffs" and requires "suitable rooms, large enough for the separate storage" and "sufficient separate refrigerated storage". It sets a capacity requirement on the premises; separate storage is a fair inference but not the text, and the scope clause is dropped. |
| eu.ingredient-storage-conditions | correct | Chapter IX, point 2: raw materials and ingredients "kept in appropriate conditions designed to prevent harmful deterioration and protect them from contamination". |
| eu.hazardous-substances-labelled | ambiguous | The title follows Chapter IX, point 8 ("adequately labelled and stored in separate and secure containers"). Rated major while eu.cleaning-chemicals-storage is critical: an unlabelled or unsecured hazardous substance is at least as direct a chemical hazard as one stored in the wrong area. |
| eu.premises-clean | correct | Annex II, Chapter I, point 1, verbatim: "kept clean and maintained in good repair and condition". |
| eu.equipment-cleaning-frequency | ambiguous | Chapter V, point 1(a): "effectively cleaned and, where necessary, disinfected", then "at a frequency sufficient to avoid any risk of contamination". The title follows the second sentence but makes disinfection unconditional. |
| eu.cleaning-chemicals-storage | correct | Annex II, Chapter I, point 10, verbatim and categorical: "not to be stored in areas where food is handled". Critical is defensible given the risk of chemical contamination. |
| eu.pest-control-procedures | ambiguous | Chapter IX, point 4: "Adequate procedures are to be in place to control pests" (PT renders pests as "parasitas" throughout, consistently). The item checks procedures, which the rubric rates major, and the domestic-animals procedures from the same point are rated major; critical fits an actual infestation, which the title does not state. |
| eu.domestic-animals-excluded | wrong | Chapter IX, point 4 requires procedures to prevent access "(or, where the competent authority so permits in special cases, to prevent such access from resulting in contamination)". The title drops that exception, which national authorities use for dogs in dining areas. |
| eu.insect-screens | correct | Annex II, Chapter II, point 1(d): openings to the outside "are, where necessary, to be fitted with insect-proof screens which can be easily removed for cleaning"; "where necessary" is kept. |
| eu.potable-water-supply | correct | Annex II, Chapter VII, point 1(a): "adequate supply of potable water, which is to be used whenever necessary to ensure that foodstuffs are not contaminated". |
| eu.ice-potable-water | ambiguous | Chapter VII, point 4 covers ice "which comes into contact with food or which may contaminate food" and allows clean water "when used to chill whole fishery products". The title drops both, so ice baths used to chill containers fall outside it and a fish display on clean-water ice would be a breach. |
| eu.ventilation | correct | Annex II, Chapter I, point 5: "suitable and sufficient means of natural or mechanical ventilation" with filters and other parts "readily accessible" for cleaning or replacement. |
| eu.floors-walls-condition | correct | Annex II, Chapter II, point 1(a) and (b): "maintained in a sound condition and be easy to clean and, where necessary, to disinfect"; the condition is kept. |
| eu.equipment-good-repair | correct | Annex II, Chapter V, point 1(b): food-contact equipment "kept in such good order, repair and condition as to minimise any risk of contamination". |
| eu.waste-closable-containers | wrong | Chapter VI, point 2 requires closable containers "unless food business operators can demonstrate to the competent authority that other types of containers or evacuation systems used are appropriate". The title drops that alternative, which covers food waste disposal units in restaurant kitchens. |
| eu.refuse-store-pest-free | wrong | Chapter VI, point 3: refuse stores "designed and managed in such a way as to enable them to be kept clean and, where necessary, free of animals and pests" (PT keeps "sempre que necessário"). The title drops "where necessary". |
| eu.waste-hygienic-disposal | ambiguous | The EN text of Chapter VI, point 4 states it ("All waste is to be eliminated in a hygienic ... way ... and is not to constitute a direct or indirect source of contamination"), but the PT version reads "Todas as águas residuais" (all waste water). Point 3 PT covers disposal of food waste only as "medidas adequadas"; the EN reading fits the chapter heading, but the PT text does not say the same. |

## Outcome

All 40 items kept; 7 wrong items fixed, 12 ambiguous items rewritten or re-rated.

- eu.haccp-records: title adds "commensurate with the business's nature and size".
- eu.establishment-registration: title states the duty to notify for registration and to notify changes; hint names the notification and the authority's acknowledgement.
- eu.haccp-training: title covers those operating relevant guides as well as the HACCP procedure; hint adds training on the national good hygiene practice guide.
- eu.personal-cleanliness: title says "every person working in" a food-handling area.
- eu.protective-clothing: title covers all who work in food-handling areas; severity minor to major, matching eu.personal-cleanliness.
- eu.ill-handlers-excluded: title covers carriers, keeping out of food areas and the likelihood-of-contamination condition; hint lists the text's examples.
- eu.cold-chain: title keeps the exception for limited periods out of temperature control with no health risk.
- eu.cold-storage-monitoring: recording is now "where necessary".
- eu.allergen-equipment-cleaning: title limited to listed allergens and food free of them; hint names Annex II to Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 and the visible-debris check.
- eu.raw-materials-acceptance: title keeps the threshold (contamination that normal sorting or preparation cannot make fit).
- eu.raw-processed-separation: title follows the text (rooms allowing separate storage, enough separate cold storage); severity critical to major, as a premises capacity requirement.
- eu.hazardous-substances-labelled: severity major to critical, matching eu.cleaning-chemicals-storage.
- eu.equipment-cleaning-frequency: disinfection is now "where necessary"; "food-contact items" follows "articles, fittings and equipment".
- eu.pest-control-procedures: severity critical to major, as a procedures item.
- eu.domestic-animals-excluded: title keeps the exception where the competent authority permits access.
- eu.ice-potable-water: title covers ice that may contaminate food and the clean-water allowance for whole fish.
- eu.waste-closable-containers: title keeps the alternative of other containers or systems shown to be suitable; hint mentions food waste disposal units.
- eu.refuse-store-pest-free: title keeps "where necessary" and "managed so they can be kept clean".
- eu.waste-hygienic-disposal: kept unchanged. The EN text of Chapter VI, point 4 says "All waste"; the PT version says "Todas as águas residuais" (waste water). The item follows the EN text and the chapter heading; an inspection of a PT establishment should cite the EN version for this point, or the reviewer of PT findings should note the divergence.
